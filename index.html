Neural Minute Publisher

Neural Minute Publisher is a private, personal tool used by the owner of the Neural Minute YouTube channel. It uploads short videos that the owner's own software has produced to that one channel, using the official YouTube Data API.

  • It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
  • It only acts on the owner's own YouTube channel, after the owner signs in and grants permission.
  • It does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data.
