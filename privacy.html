Privacy Policy

Neural Minute Publisher. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who uses this app

Neural Minute Publisher is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Neural Minute YouTube channel to upload the owner's own videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's official YouTube APIs to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel;
  • read basic information about that channel and its videos (to confirm uploads succeeded);
  • read YouTube Analytics for that channel (views, watch time and similar figures) to plan future videos.

How data is stored and shared

  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not access any other person's account or personal data.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
